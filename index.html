Ph.D. Student, HKUST CSE · Research Intern, Tencent Hy

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Kai Chen in the iSING Lab. Previously, I received my M.Phil. degree from HKUST CSE, also advised by Prof. Kai Chen, and my B.S. degree in Applied Mathematics from Northwestern Polytechnical University.

I am currently a research intern on the RL Team of the Foundation Model Department at Tencent Hy (formerly Tencent Hunyuan), as part of the Tencent Qingyun Program. My research focuses on long-horizon agent reinforcement learning, as well as algorithms and infrastructure for agent RL training.

When Do Larger Batches Help Scale LLM Reinforcement Learning?

Ziniu Li, Jinbo Wang, Guanhua Huang, Feiyuan Zhang, Pengbo Li, Alex Chen

Tencent Hy · arXiv:2608.29296, 2026

A larger batch shortens wall-clock time-to-target only when its throughput gain exceeds its samples-to-target penalty. In GRPO, pairing larger batches with learning-rate retuning reduces time-to-target by up to 29%.
